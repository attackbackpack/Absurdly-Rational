# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Readers looking for physician-led, evidence-based commentary on health, policy, clinical practice, critical thinking, and hospital life, plus listeners and visitors who prefer podcasts or humor.

## Product Purpose

Absurdly Rational is the public home for Casey Husser, MD's writing, podcast work, guest appearances, and memes. It helps visitors choose the format and subject that suits them, then continue to the original publishing platform.

## Positioning

The site combines clinical experience, critical reasoning, and an intentionally irreverent voice to challenge unsupported claims without presenting itself as humorless institutional communication.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a static Jekyll/Liquid, CSS, and JavaScript project published at `https://absurdlyrational.com/`; editable content lives in structured JSON under `_data/`.
- Pages CMS is the protected editor for the repository owner and invited collaborators. It exposes labeled text, links, image uploads, alt text, constrained image fit/focus choices, visibility, and safe content ordering without exposing templates or layout code.
- Uploaded images live under `assets/uploads/`, are validated by Node, and can be optimized through a Pages CMS GitHub Actions media action using Sharp/WebP conversion.
- Pages CMS edits are saved to a persistent `editor` branch, rendered under `/preview/` with a visible draft banner and search-engine blocking, and published only after an owner merges the review request into `main`.
- Selected readings link out to Substack rather than reproducing full articles.
- The podcast page links to Spotify and to verified guest-appearance destinations.
- The meme bank supports simple thumbnail browsing and previous/next navigation after a meme is opened.
- External destinations must not be invented. Generic verified destinations may stand in until exact article or episode URLs are supplied.
- Search metadata must describe real page content and must not promise rankings.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Absurdly Rational.
- Author identity: Casey Husser, MD.
- Voice: evidence-based, unapologetically irreverent, absurdist, funny, and direct.
- Existing rooster mark at `assets/rooster-light.png` remains the identifying asset.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing homepage copy and public platform links in `index.html`.
- Four reading themes supplied by the site owner: evidence versus hype; policy and practice; critical thinking and decision-making; and hospital life and philosophical observations.
- Public Absurdly Rational Substack and Spotify show destinations.
- Stanford's Future of Decision-Making project page and Be a Health Architect website are public, but the exact Casey Husser guest-episode URLs have not yet been confirmed.
- Reading artwork references are present in the structured content; no local uploaded meme images are present yet.

## Product Principles

- Make the evidence legible without sanding off the humor.
- Let visitors choose by topic and format immediately.
- Send visitors to the original source with clear expectations.
- Keep factual claims and external links verifiable.
- Preserve fast, accessible use on phone and desktop.
